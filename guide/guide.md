# My Module

**Say here, in one sentence, what My Module does for you.**

Every change is saved the moment you make it.

## Counting

Click **Add one** to add one to the count, and **Start again** to set it back to 0. The Widget on the Home shows the count: click it to open My Module.

## Your data

The count is kept in your Tessello space, on your computer, and nowhere else. Uninstalling the Module deletes it.
